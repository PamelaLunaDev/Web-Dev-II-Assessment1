About Paris

Paris is the capital of France and one of the most famous travel destinations in the world.

The city is known for its art, architecture, history, fashion and food. From the Eiffel Tower to the beautiful River Seine, Paris has something special for every visitor.

Paris is my dream destination because I love exploring new cultures and discovering beautiful places.

Would you like to know an interesting fact about this beautiful city?

Paris is often called the City of Light, a nickname connected to its history as a centre of learning and its early adoption of street lighting.
